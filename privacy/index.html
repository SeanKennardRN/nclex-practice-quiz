Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurse Acumen Coaching LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the NCLEX Triumph Accelerator Discord application (the “Service”).

1. Information the Service may process

When you use the Service, we may process information supplied by Discord, such as your Discord user ID, username, server ID, channel ID, roles, and the commands or interactions you submit. We may also process information you voluntarily provide through the Service, along with basic technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot it.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide requested features, manage access, personalize educational interactions, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, understand Service performance, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sensitive information

Do not submit patient information, protected health information, financial account information, government identification numbers, passwords, or other sensitive personal information through the Service. The Service is not intended to receive or store clinical records.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that support hosting, security, analytics, or Service operation; by Discord according to its own policies; or when disclosure is required by law, needed to protect rights and safety, or connected with a business transfer.

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including security, troubleshooting, educational administration, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by data type and operational need. We may aggregate or de-identify information so it no longer identifies an individual.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may stop using the Service at any time. Server administrators may remove the application from a server. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information associated with you; we may need to verify your identity, and applicable law may limit some requests.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

9. Third-party services

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord’s privacy practices. Links or integrations with other services are governed by those services’ own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when changes are posted.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be submitted through the official Nurse Acumen Coaching website at nac.llc.
